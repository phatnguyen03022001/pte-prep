# Private Materials

This directory is a **local-only personal study area** for third-party PTE materials that the learner lawfully obtains through provider-authorized access.

Examples include:

- prediction or forecast PDFs;
- question banks;
- practice images;
- audio or video supplied for personal study;
- notes exported from a paid or free preparation service when local storage is permitted.

## Git policy

Everything under this directory is ignored by Git **except this README**.

Do not force-add downloaded third-party assets. They are not part of the canonical reusable dataset and must not be copied into numbered `media/` items, the application repository, releases, or other distributable outputs unless separate reusable rights are documented.

## Suggested local layout

```text
private-materials/
├── README.md
└── <provider>/
    └── <YYYY-MM>/
        └── <downloaded files>
```

Keep provider/file names intact when practical so the source is easy to identify later.

## Study use

Use these materials to increase prompt variety and practise timed responses. Prediction or “high-frequency” labels are third-party coaching signals, not Pearson scoring authority and not a guarantee that an item will appear in the exam.

Items already seen here are **not unseen**. Do not reuse them in Mock A, Mock B, diagnostics, or other held-out checks that are meant to measure transfer to new material.

## Access rules

Use only normal provider-authorized access. Do not bypass paywalls, authentication, DRM, CAPTCHAs, download restrictions, or other technical controls.
